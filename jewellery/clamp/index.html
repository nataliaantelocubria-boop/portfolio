PARITY / 01

CLAMP

2026 · NECKPIECE · INDUSTRIAL / ORNAMENTAL · OPEN COLLECTION

01 — QUESTION

WHAT MAKES US PERCEIVE ONE MATERIAL AS MORE VALUABLE THAN ANOTHER?

PARITY brings together components associated with different contexts and cultural hierarchies of value. Industrial hardware, anodised aluminium rings, a metallic structure and a faceted stone keep their different associations. The assembly asks why an ordinary fixing and an ornamental element are read as belonging to different levels of value.

The hardware remains recognisable. Its role changes when it holds the stone and becomes part of the neckpiece.

02 — COMPONENT + COMPONENT + COMPONENT

THE CONNECTION REFUSES TO PRETEND IT IS ANYTHING ELSE.

The faceted pink stone sits directly within visible industrial hardware. The screw thread and nuts remain legible. Pink/fuchsia anodised aluminium rings repeat around a curved metallic structure, using colour to connect elements whose material associations remain deliberately different.

03 — ASSEMBLY / PERCEPTION

Pink/fuchsia anodised aluminium rings repeat around the silver-coloured structure. The faceted pink stone, screw thread and nuts remain visible rather than being hidden inside a conventional setting.

The process sheet and component drawing place the ornamental element beside the fixing and the repeated rings. The intervention is in how these components are brought together, and how that assembly changes their perception.

The stone and the hardware do not arrive with the same perceived value. CLAMP makes them occupy the same object.
