CLAMP

2026 · NECKPIECE · INDUSTRIAL / ORNAMENTAL · OPEN COLLECTION

01 — QUESTION

WHAT MAKES US PERCEIVE ONE MATERIAL AS MORE VALUABLE THAN ANOTHER?

PARITY brings together components associated with different contexts and cultural hierarchies of value. Industrial hardware, anodised aluminium, metal structures and faceted stones are allowed to retain their own visual language.

The intention is not to disguise an ordinary component until it resembles traditional jewellery.

02 — COMPONENT + COMPONENT + COMPONENT

THE CONNECTION REFUSES TO PRETEND IT IS ANYTHING ELSE.

The faceted pink stone sits directly within visible industrial hardware. The screw thread and nuts remain legible. Pink/fuchsia anodised aluminium rings repeat around a curved metallic structure, using colour to connect elements whose material associations remain deliberately different.

03 — CURRENT TECHNICAL RECORD

Confirmed material: pink/fuchsia anodised aluminium.

Visible elements: silver-coloured metallic structure, visible hardware and faceted pink/fuchsia stone.

Pending confirmation: exact stone, tubular metal, hardware material, fabrication and assembly techniques, dimensions and context.

The stone and the hardware do not arrive with the same perceived value. CLAMP makes them occupy the same object.
