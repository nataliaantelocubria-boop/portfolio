FALLA

2025

FALLA began with agates whose veins, colours and outlines differed from one another. Cutting, shaping and polishing them by hand preserved those differences rather than making every stone identical.

The most irregular agate sits at the centre. Brass supports it through a visible structure; rings, settings and agate discs create a more ordered sequence on either side. The composition moves from an uneven focal point towards repeated elements.

Brass works as both a connection and a support. Its geometry links stones with different shapes into a necklace that can be assembled and worn. The development sketches and drawings record that construction.
