GEMHYPE

2026 · Instagram analysis & strategic opportunities
Marketing · Social media · Visual communication

01 — THE PROBLEM

A SHOWCASE WITH LITTLE OF THE PROCESS BEHIND IT.

The Instagram audit identified high visual quality, regular posting, clear B2B positioning and use of reels. The main observation was that products were more visible than the expertise, processes and people behind them.

02 — THE OPPORTUNITY

MAKE EXISTING EXPERTISE EASIER TO SEE.

The proposal builds on the account’s existing identity and sustainability messaging. Cutting, quality control and educational gemology provide subjects that can explain how stones are selected and worked, alongside the product images.

03 — THE STRATEGY

STORYTELLING. STRUCTURE. PARTICIPATION.

Recommendations combine clearer profile imagery, organised highlights, simplified links and consistent English with process-led content. Story polls, Q&A, collaborations and comment interaction are proposed ways to invite participation.

04 — THE SYSTEM

GIVE EACH FORMAT A JOB.

The existing mockup brings the proposal into a profile, carousel post and reel. Why Shape Changes Everything explains the effect of cutting; Under the Loupe makes examination and expertise visible. Other directions in the PDF develop transformation, heritage and collaboration across a coherent content system.

05 — THE DIRECTION / OUTCOME

A STRATEGIC PROPOSAL, NOT A MEASURED CAMPAIGN.

The work connects an observation about the account to recommendations and visual content directions. The intended outcome is to make expertise and process more visible. Implementation, engagement gains and business results are not claimed.
