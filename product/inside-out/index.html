INSIDE OUT

2026 · Exhibition project · Two-day workshop
Identity, introspection and relationships through play

01 — IDEA

A GAME THAT LOOKS BACK

Inside Out reinterprets the familiar structure of Parcheesi as a tool for self-observation. The objective is no longer simply to reach the finish line: the way each person thinks, feels, pursues goals and relates to others becomes part of the game.

The idea is not to win,
but to discover what the way
you play reveals about you.

02 — MATERIAL

REFLECTION / REVELATION

Two mirrors place the player visually inside the object. The hand-engraved glass board works together with internal light: when the light is off, reflection dominates; when activated, the hidden board appears.

MATERIAL LANGUAGE

Black rigid enclosure · two mirrored surfaces · hand-engraved glass · internal lighting · hand-formed coloured metal wire pieces · custom icon-based die.

03 — OUTCOME

IDENTITY IN MOTION

The conventional pawns are replaced by manually formed wire pieces. Each family develops a different geometry and colour, turning the pieces into physical extensions of the four dimensions of identity.

Playing with others becomes a way of looking at yourself.

04 — DEVELOPMENT

ADAPTING THE PROCESS.

Constraint: the planned glass-engraving process became unavailable during a project conceptualised and made in approximately two days.

Decision: I engraved the board manually with a Dremel. Tool limitations during construction also meant resolving some drilling and other operations by hand.

Result: the engraved lines still work with the internal light to reveal the board. The process changed while the reflection-and-revelation idea remained.

05 — TECHNICAL

HOLDING THE BOARD. KEEPING ACCESS.

Constraint: the glass needed secure support, and the lighting needed to remain accessible for maintenance.

Decision: the board is fitted and held by a fixing rather than simply glued in place. The screw connections and structure were developed with strength and disassembly in mind.

Result: loosening the relevant connection releases the glass piece, allowing the object to be opened and the internal lighting accessed.
