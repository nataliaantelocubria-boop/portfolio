ABOUT ME

I am a Product Design student and junior designer with a particular interest in the relationship between materials, form and making.

My background combines product design with hands-on experience in contemporary jewellery, glass and craft processes. I enjoy moving between different scales and disciplines, from objects and functional products to jewellery and more experimental pieces.

Working directly with materials is an important part of the way I design. Processes such as metalworking, glass, enamelling, thermoforming and model making have taught me to understand design not only through its final form, but also through how an object is made, how materials behave and how ideas evolve during the process.

My approach combines conceptual thinking with practical development. I am particularly interested in projects where material, function and identity come together, and where experimentation can lead to unexpected solutions.

I am currently looking for opportunities to continue learning and developing professionally within product design, jewellery, contemporary craft and related creative fields.
