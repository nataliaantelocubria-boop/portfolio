ABOUT ME

I am a Product Design student. Before studying objects as products, I approached them through engineering, glass and jewellery.

Engineering studies taught me to read drawings, work with systems and ask how an object could be built. Glass brought those questions into the workshop: a process changes the material, and that change affects transparency, light and form.

Jewellery made construction more immediate. Working with metal and stone means resolving small joints, surfaces and connections, then seeing how they sit on the body. A detail has to hold together as well as look right.

Product design brings these ways of working into the same project. In INSIDE OUT, access to the lighting and a removable glass board had to be resolved alongside the game. In STACK, hand positions, pouring and lid handling were studied alongside the proportions of the family.

Making can also change the starting idea. In LATENT, cutting, faceting and thermal contrast produced information that became part of the ring. I look for a reason for an object to take its particular form: what it does, how it is made and what it means to the person using it.

I am looking for opportunities to keep learning within product design and to bring this workshop experience into the development of objects.
