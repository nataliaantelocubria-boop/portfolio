ABOUT ME

I am a Product Design student, curious about how things work and how they are made.

I like moving between a drawing and the workshop: understanding an object, working with its materials and finding where that knowledge can be useful next.

BACKGROUND

What each discipline
has added.

Engineering studies gave me a technical starting point: drawings, systems and the question of how an object can be built. Working with glass brought those questions into the material itself, through heat, transformation, transparency and light.

Jewellery and gemology added another scale. With metal and stone, small differences in a joint, an edge or a surface change how a piece is constructed and worn.

Product design connects this knowledge with function, context, users and manufacturing. I use what I learn in one field to approach a question in another, rather than treating each discipline as a separate practice.

Learning something new often begins with a specific question. What interests me afterwards is where else that understanding can be applied.
